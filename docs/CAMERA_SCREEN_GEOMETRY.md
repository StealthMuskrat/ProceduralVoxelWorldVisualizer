# Camera screen geometry — unvalidated implementation

Specification 16.1.2 requires right-drag to pan relative to camera orientation, while 16.1.3 assigns world X/Z and Y movement to middle-drag. The previous right-pan formula rotated only around yaw and always moved on the ground plane. The shared 3D Noise/World camera now uses its complete orthonormal screen basis, including pitch: a horizontal grab moves the pivot opposite camera right, and a downward grab moves it along camera up. Existing distance-based sensitivity remains `distance × .0015` per pointer pixel. The eye and pivot translate together, with unchanged yaw/pitch/distance. Middle-drag retains its separate press-time world-plane lock.

The same basis now constructs picking rays analytically in double precision. Normalized OpenGL X/Y and aspect scale camera right/up by the tangent of the existing vertical FOV. The direction is normalized independently of absolute coordinates. The ray starts on the rendered near plane, preserving clipping rather than selecting an invisible cell between the eye and that plane. FOV, near and far projection constants are shared with the unchanged float GPU matrix policy. The render-origin parameter remains source-compatible but cannot alter an absolute world ray. This avoids deriving a direction by subtracting two float-unprojected points at the very distant far plane.

Orbit gesture ownership, total-displacement previews, final-release movement, Escape rollback, external navigation authority, context-click thresholds and free-fly lifecycle remain authoritative. Camera movement is view state: the committed project, private Properties/raw seed input, history and preferences stay with their existing owners. Central visualization Help now explains right-drag versus the two middle-drag planes.

## Deferred validation definitions

Nine `CameraGeometryTest` definitions cover exact level/pitched pan, orthogonality, eye/pivot translation, distance sensitivity, reverse movement, center/off-axis near-plane rays, aspect, signed large coordinates, render-origin invariance, orbit/free-fly agreement, 81 forward-projection observations, clipped-cell exclusion and exact voxel selection. Two added `OrbitGestureTest` definitions cover pitched Escape restoration and frame-partition/final-release equivalence; the prior right-pan expectation is corrected to the screen plane.

The existing production `OrbitInputProbe` now defines level and two pitched native right-drag routes, while retaining outside capture, context separation, middle axes, focus/tab/Escape and real Inspector picking. Its version-2 receipts, complete before/after project/preferences files, retained private noise draft and invalid raw seed feed the updated independent World checker. Historical evidence files are not rewritten.

`CameraGeometryProbe`, reached by the existing World picking harness, defines 144 raster/DDA observations: 36 combinations of three signed origins (0/±2,000,000,000), three orientations, before/after screen pan and orbit/free-fly. Each uses three pixels on a single exact rendered voxel and one clear miss: 108 hits and 36 misses. Independent `verify_camera_geometry.py` reconstructs the screen basis, pan, near-plane origin, normalized ray and slab intersection from fixed formulas, with explicit native float conversion for NDC/aspect/FOV. It compares real readback colors and voxel coordinates. Engine-only `CameraNavigationConsumer` defines 162 constant-field observations and 576 exact filled/EMPTY cells across both native saved documents, root sizes 2/4, signed X/Z and in/out-of-project Y chunks. Future Linux/Windows manual commands include these definitions and existing World picking artifacts retain the output.

No compiler, JUnit, native probe, independent consumer, Python checker or GitHub workflow ran for this batch. The validated baseline remains 922 tests / 120 suites. Main is the sole branch and the manual-only testing pause remains. GPU matrices still use mesh-relative floats; this scoped change does not certify arbitrary camera/mesh separations, all ray-edge cases, physical input/DPI, real GPU/Windows 11 performance, full GUI acceptance or final specification conformance.
