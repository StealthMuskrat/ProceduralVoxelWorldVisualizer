## Observer isolation

Runtime diagnostics are observations of an already determined failure. A listener previously throwing RuntimeException was ignored, while an Error escaped. During region failure recording that could abort a CONTINUE stream, skip later listeners and replace the useful terminal outcome with an observer failure. During queue rejection it could escape generateChunkAsync after its rejected future had already been completed.

The engine now owns a small diagnostic registry. Each report snapshots insertion-ordered entries under the registry gate, then invokes callbacks outside that gate. Every callback Throwable is contained, later eligible listeners are attempted, and the original Diagnostic and host failures remain unmodified. No recursive diagnostic is emitted for an observer fault. Generation, consumer acknowledgements, failure counters/policy and futures retain authority over their existing outcomes. Callback execution stays on the reporting thread: listeners must return promptly and may still block their caller. This does not promise isolation from a blocked host, process termination or JVM resource exhaustion.

Existing addDiagnosticListener(Consumer<Diagnostic>) remains void. Repeated registration of one instance produces repeated observations. The additive removeDiagnosticListener(Consumer<Diagnostic>) returns whether it removed any registration and removes all registrations of that exact instance. Removal does not invoke host equals/hashCode. Retain the registered Consumer instance to remove it; creating a new method reference is not an identity match. Null registration/removal rejects.

Removal and shutdown clear entry callback references as well as registry membership. A captured entry is skipped until a reporter has claimed its callback by reading the reference; a claimed callback may finish after removal or closure. Removal never waits for host code. Registering during a dispatch takes effect on the next report. Removing and re-registering creates a new entry and does not revive an old captured entry. Callback self-removal, removal of a later listener and engine closure from a callback are permitted.

Engine shutdown closes the registry before context/worker/profiler cleanup, revoking remaining unclaimed callbacks and releasing owned callback references. Repeated closure is harmless. Registration after registry shutdown rejects with IllegalStateException; removal remains usable and returns false for already released entries. A registration concurrent with shutdown either precedes registry closure and is cleared or rejects. Reports after shutdown have no callback effects. Engine/context cleanup and immutable retained snapshots otherwise retain their existing contracts.
